# 💫 About Me:
I am a Computer Science undergraduate at UPES (Dehradun), specializing in Artificial Intelligence and Machine Learning, graduating in May 2026. I have hands-on experience across deep learning, machine learning, and full-stack development, having built projects like DeepRetinopathy (a CNN-based model detecting diabetic retinopathy from retina scans), FIFA StatX (a predictive ML model for player and match performance), and PragyanM (a full-stack, cloud-deployed platform for secure research collaboration). I completed a full-stack development internship at IBM, working with React.js and Passport.js, and a social internship focused on community engagement. My technical skill set spans Python, TensorFlow, Scikit-learn, React.js, Node.js, MongoDB, and AWS, along with growing knowledge of LLMs, RAG, and Agentic AI. I am proactive, self-driven, and comfortable debugging and shipping projects end-to-end rather than just following tutorials.
